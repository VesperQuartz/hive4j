Workload check for Q3. Two projects need design time at once: the onboarding redesign Sam wants before the paid cohort, and the console refresh that the support team keeps asking for. I can do one of them well, not both, and the design system work would slip either way.

Decision: hire a contract designer for ten weeks, starting July 6. The scope is the console refresh and the empty states, not the onboarding flow, because onboarding depends on decisions Sam has not made yet. The contractor works in the same Figma library and joins the Monday design review.

Budget is €14,000 for the ten weeks, which comes out of the Q3 contractor line Priya already approved. I will run the interview loop with Lena, and the first two weeks are a paid trial on the console navigation. If the trial does not work, we stop there and I take the console refresh back in August.
