Hosting review for the beta. We need preview URLs per pull request, a staging environment that mirrors production, and as little operations work as possible while the team is six people.

Vercel won on preview deployments. Every pull request gets a URL, the design partners can click it, and Aisha can review a change before it merges. The edge network also fixed the latency Tom saw from the first cohort in Australia, without us choosing a region or adding a CDN configuration.

Atlas, the billing service, stays on a small container for now because it needs a long-running worker. Marco flagged two limits to watch: serverless function duration and egress. The export job is already close to the 300 second ceiling, and the first large customer will push it over. We will revisit hosting when that happens.
