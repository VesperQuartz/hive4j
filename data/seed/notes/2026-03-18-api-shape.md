Sam brought three design partner requests to the API discussion. All three wanted to pull events and user segments from Rill into their own tools, and two of them asked for curl examples before they would sign.

We chose REST with an OpenAPI 3.1 spec. The spec is the contract: we generate the SDK, the reference docs, and the request validation from it. GraphQL was on the table, but the partners do not have GraphQL clients and the queries they need are shallow. tRPC was tempting for internal speed, but it would not give external partners a language-neutral surface.

The plan is one spec with two audiences. Public endpoints live under `/v1` and are versioned. Internal endpoints can move faster, but they still go through the same router until we have a reason to split. Marco will write the first spec and Lena will own the generated client.
