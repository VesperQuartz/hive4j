First team sync for Rill, the customer analytics workspace we are building for small SaaS teams. Six of us in the room: Priya, Marco, Lena, Sam, Aisha, and Tom. The goal for the quarter is a working product with ten design partners, not a platform.

We agreed to keep everything in one repository with a Turborepo pipeline. The console, the marketing site, and the shared type package change together, and a single lockfile has already saved us from version drift. We also settled on Tailwind v4 with one token file, because Aisha needs to move between Figma and the browser without a translation layer.

For authentication we picked Auth0. Nobody wants to run identity infrastructure in the first quarter, and the free tier covers the design partners. Priya will own the integration and Marco will wire the session into the API.
