Q3 retro with the whole team. The theme was migration: identity, database, API, analytics, and hosting all moved in twelve weeks. That is too many at once, and the retro is honest about it.

What worked: the parallel-run pattern. Okta next to Auth0, Neon next to RDS, PostHog next to Segment, Fly next to Vercel. Every migration had a week where both paths were live, and no customer saw a hard cutover. The preview database was the single biggest quality improvement; Lena's schema reviews stopped fighting over staging data.

What did not: the API refactor. oRPC landed for new screens, but the old REST handlers are still there and the console calls both. We shipped the new thing without retiring the old one, which is the same mistake as the analytics migration. The design contractor trial also ended early; the console refresh is back with Aisha.

Q4 rule: one migration at a time, and the old path gets a deletion date in the same pull request that adds the new one. Priya will track the deletion dates in the roadmap review.
