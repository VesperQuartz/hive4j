Support review before the first paid cohort. The ticket volume is 40 to 60 a month, mostly onboarding questions, and the peak is between 09:00 and 16:00 CET. The two enterprise prospects asked about 24/7 coverage, but neither has an incident profile that needs it today.

Decision: weekday support from 09:00 to 17:00 CET, staffed by Sam and rotating engineers. Outside those hours the console links to a status page and the on-call pager stays for incidents, not questions. Customers who need a guaranteed response can buy an escalation plan with a one-hour target.

Tom will put the escalation plan on the pricing page after the paid cohort lands. Priya asked that we track the first response time and the percentage of tickets solved in the first reply, because those are the numbers that tell us whether the rotation is working. The plan gets reviewed at the end of Q3.
